Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Website packages start at KSh 2,000. The final price depends on the agreed features, integrations and project requirements.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. The websites are designed to adapt to smartphones, tablets and desktop screens.

How long does development take?

Simple projects may take a few working days. The delivery timeline is agreed after reviewing the project requirements and receiving the necessary content.

Can customers order through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp buttons and prefilled enquiry or ordering messages can be included in suitable packages.

Can you connect M-Pesa payments?

Payment integration can be discussed as a separate feature. It depends on payment provider approval, account access, technical requirements and testing.

Do you provide maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance and technical support can be arranged under a separate agreement.
